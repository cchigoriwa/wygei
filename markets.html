Products and markets

Fresh vegetables grown under solar drip irrigation, sold to Harare buyers on agreed grade, volume and delivery days.

What we plan to grow Projection

Tomatoes

0.5 ha, about 8 tonnes a year. Graded and packed in crates for traders, retailers and restaurants.

Onions

0.75 ha, about 7 tonnes a year. Cured to store well, so we can sell when prices are better.

Potatoes

0.75 ha, about 5 tonnes a year. Sorted by size and sold in bags to wholesalers and institutions.

Planned volumes for the pilot. The crop mix will follow buyer demand.

Who we sell to

  • Traders

    Wholesale traders at Mbare Musika for volume and quick payment.

  • Retail

    Supermarkets and greengrocers needing consistent grade and packing.

  • Food service

    Restaurants and caterers buying weekly, by standing order.

  • Institutions

    Schools, clinics and canteens with steady, predictable demand.

How we will supply

  • Agreed first

    Price per kilogram, grade, packaging, delivery days and payment terms in writing before each planting.

  • Spread risk

    No single buyer takes more than 40% of sales.

  • Recorded

    Every order, delivery, rejection and payment tracked, so buyers can rely on us.

  • Status

    Buyer letters of intent are being sought. No supply agreements have been signed yet.

  • Regional

    SADC sales only once local supply is steady and export pays after transport and standards.
