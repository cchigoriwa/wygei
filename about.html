About us

WYGEI is an agribusiness with a social mission, based in Waterfalls, Harare. We grow and sell fresh produce, and we use the profit to create work for young people.

From water and energy to food

WYGEI began by drilling boreholes, installing solar power and training young people to do that work. Reliable water is the input most small farms lack, so we are now putting it to use on our own farm, growing crops for paying buyers.

We are building WYGEI as a business first: production leads to sales, sales to profit, and profit to growth and jobs.

Core business

Agriculture

Commercial vegetable production planned against confirmed demand.

Competitive advantage

Green energy

Our own borehole and solar skills give the farm dependable, low-cost water.

Social impact

Youth

Paid jobs, training and out-grower plots for young women and men aged 16 to 35.
