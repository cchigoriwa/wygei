# WYGEI baseline assumptions

The single source of figures for the website, the 10-slide pitch deck and the one-page Growth & Job Creation Pitch. Change a figure here first, then update all three.

Status labels used in every document:

- **Actual**: true today, as stated by WYGEI.
- **Target**: what the pilot aims to achieve.
- **Projection**: illustrative financial or production figures.
- **Estimate**: funding amounts pending supplier quotations.

Production estimates, selling prices, costs and investment requirements are illustrative and remain subject to validation. They are not results or forecasts.
