Youth empowerment

Paid work, practical training and a route to running your own plot, for young women and men aged 16 to 35.

Jobs the farm can pay for

4paid farm jobs in year one, funded from sales
20young people trained on the farm

Targets, not results yet. At least half of the young people we recruit will be young women.

What you get

  • Hands-on training in irrigated farming, solar and borehole upkeep
  • A chance at paid work on our farm and projects
  • Support to grow your own plot and sell through WYGEI

Who can join

  • Aged 16 to 35, in and around Harare
  • Especially young people not in work, school or training
  • Ready to commit to the full training period
