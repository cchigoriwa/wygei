The first 12 months

  1. Months 1–3
    Validate.

    Confirm site, water tests, crop mix and buyers. Recruit the first youth group.

  2. Months 4–6
    Produce.

    Install solar drip, train on the farm, plant in staggered batches and record every cost.

  3. Months 7–9
    Sell.

    Harvest, grade, deliver and get paid. Compare real margins with the plan.

  4. Months 10–12
    Expand.

    Grow the crops and buyers that worked. Prepare out-grower plots for year two.
